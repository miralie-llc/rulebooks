# Legal and quality analogs for RuleBooks vocabulary

This crosswalk compares the [RuleBooks vocabulary](vocabulary.md) with terms from U.S. law, agency enforcement, parliamentary procedure, and quality control. The parallels identify distinctions worth preserving. Each source retains its own definitions and authority.

This is supplementary terminology research. It supplies neither game rules nor legal requirements for RuleBooks, and it is not an implementation backlog or an executable semantic contract. Use the [current product model](../product/data-and-versioning.md) for scope and entity decisions. Verify the cited source edition when adopting an example into reviewed content.

## Rules and authority

| RuleBooks term | Established analog | What to carry over |
|---|---|---|
| **Rule, ruling, adjudication** | The U.S. Administrative Procedure Act distinguishes a rule with future effect, the process of making a rule, and adjudication, the process of formulating an order. | Keep the rule, the process for applying it to a case, and the resulting decision separately citable. [5 U.S.C. § 551](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section551) |
| **Publication, effective date** | Federal rulemaking distinguishes publication from the date a rule takes effect. | Keep the source publication date, effective period, and RuleBooks revision separate. [5 U.S.C. § 553(d)](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section553) |
| **Interpretation, guidance, requirement** | FDA describes guidance as its interpretation or policy and explains its generally nonbinding status. A guidance document can cite an independently binding requirement. | Record what an interpretation says and what authority, if any, makes it binding. [FDA guidance overview](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/guidances) |
| **Default, precedence, local rule** | Robert’s Rules treats its rules as defaults where applicable law, bylaws, or adopted special rules of order do not provide otherwise. | A tournament supplement or house rule needs a scope and a basis for taking precedence. Local adoption and permission to override are separate facts. [Robert’s Rules FAQ](https://robertsrules.com/frequently-asked-questions/) |
| **Exception, authorized deviation, suspension** | Federal procurement allows documented authorization of individual deviations. Robert’s Rules provides a procedure for suspending rules where suspension is permitted. | An exception already in the rule can apply on its own terms. An approved departure needs a decision, an authority, and a scope. Temporary suspension and lasting amendment have different effects. [FAR 1.403](https://www.acquisition.gov/far/1.403) · [Robert’s Rules Official Interpretations](https://robertsrules.com/official-interpretations/) |

## Observations, objections, and findings

| RuleBooks term | Established analog | What to carry over |
|---|---|---|
| **Evidence observation, allegation, finding** | FDA Form 483 records inspectional observations for agency evaluation. EPA describes notices that can follow discovery of an alleged violation. | An observed event, a claim that it breached a rule, and a decision that a breach occurred are different statements. A notice or report can precede a decision. [FDA Form 483 FAQ](https://www.fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/inspection-references/fda-form-483-frequently-asked-questions) · [EPA enforcement FAQ](https://echo.epa.gov/resources/general-info/echo-faq) |
| **Claim, objection, point of order, appeal** | Federal Rule of Evidence 103 distinguishes a timely objection that preserves an issue from the ruling on it. Robert’s Rules distinguishes raising a point of order from appealing the chair’s ruling. | Record who can raise an issue, its deadline, and who rules on it. An objection or claim raises the issue. An appeal seeks review of a decision already made. [Federal Rule of Evidence 103](https://www.uscourts.gov/sites/default/files/document/federal-rules-of-evidence.pdf) · [Robert’s Rules 12th edition overview](https://robertsrules.com/books/newly-revised-12th-edition/) |
| **Missed deadline, waiver, forfeiture** | In *United States v. Olano*, the Supreme Court distinguished failing to assert a right on time from intentionally giving up a known right. | Use **claim window closed** for the game record where the time to act has passed. Reserve **waiver** for a supported intentional relinquishment. Keep **game forfeit** for loss of the game under the applicable rules. [*United States v. Olano*, 507 U.S. 725, 733 (1993)](https://www.govinfo.gov/app/details/USREPORTS-507/USREPORTS-507-725) |
| **Play stands, continuing breach** | Federal Civil Rule 61 limits when errors justify disturbing a judgment. Robert’s Rules gives examples of an untimely point of order and a continuing breach that can still be raised. | Whether a breach occurred, whether review is still available, and whether the outcome changes are separate questions. Each system supplies its own grounds and deadlines. [Federal Civil Rule 61](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?edition=prelim&num=0&req=granuleid%3AUSC-prelim-title28a-node89-node164-rule61) · [Robert’s Rules Official Interpretations 2006-17 and 2006-18](https://robertsrules.com/official-interpretations/) |

## Nonconformance and disposition

| RuleBooks term | Established analog | What to carry over |
|---|---|---|
| **Deviation, discrepancy, nonconformance** | U.S. drug manufacturing regulations call for recording and justifying deviations from written procedures and investigating unexplained discrepancies or specification failures. Federal procurement addresses nonconforming supplies and services. | Use **deviation** for the departure and **nonconformance** for a mismatch against a stated specification. Keep **infraction** for a breach of an applicable play or conduct provision. Specify **authorized deviation** when a rule system has approved one. [21 CFR § 211.100(b)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-211/subpart-F/section-211.100) · [21 CFR § 211.192](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-211/subpart-J/section-211.192) · [FAR 46.407](https://www.acquisition.gov/far/46.407) |
| **Remedy, penalty, sanction** | EPA describes compliance orders that can require corrective work with or without penalties. The APA's definition of sanction includes penalties and other restrictive actions. | Record restoration of play separately from an imposed penalty or broader disciplinary action. Both can arise from one ruling. [EPA enforcement overview](https://www.epa.gov/enforcement/basic-information-enforcement) · [5 U.S.C. § 551(10)](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section551) |
| **Disposition, rejection, conditional acceptance** | Federal procurement rules provide for rejection, correction or replacement, and authorized acceptance or conditional acceptance of nonconforming supplies or services. | Record the final treatment of the case independently of the original mismatch. The game analog is an illegal move that stands under the applicable procedure. Expiry of a claim window and affirmative acceptance are distinct grounds. [FAR 46.407](https://www.acquisition.gov/far/46.407) · [FIDE Laws of Chess, Appendix A.5.2](https://handbook.fide.com/chapter/E012023) |
| **Correction, corrective action** | ICH Q10, published as FDA guidance, uses corrective action for addressing the cause of a detected nonconformity to prevent recurrence. | Restoring a position corrects this incident. Changing the equipment or procedure that repeatedly produces bad positions addresses recurrence. [FDA and ICH Q10, § 3.2.2 and glossary](https://www.fda.gov/downloads/Drugs/Guidances/ucm073517.pdf) |

## Applying the crosswalk

In a rapid chess game under FIDE A.5.2, a participant can report what they saw, identify the move as allegedly illegal, and make a timely claim. The arbiter can also act on an observed violation within the specified window. If the opponent replies without either step, the game can continue with the move standing. That disposition follows from the procedure. It does not establish intent or give the move the status of a permitted action. [FIDE Laws of Chess, Appendix A.5.2](https://handbook.fide.com/chapter/E012023)

The useful borrowed terms include **evidence observation**, **finding**, **claim window**, **appeal**, **authorized deviation**, **sanction**, and **disposition**. Keep *tactical infraction* as a description of a deliberate tactic and preserve each game's own labels for fouls, misconduct, and cheating. Formal-game observation means participant-visible information and is a separate concept.
